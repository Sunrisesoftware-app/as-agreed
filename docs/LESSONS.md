## 27.10.2026: an idea screen scores criteria we had not written to

### The idea was not shortlisted, and the criteria arrived only with the rejection

The organisers named their screening criteria only in the rejection email: relevance, impact,
innovation, complexity and implementation, and product integration. No per-idea feedback was given,
so what follows are hypotheses, not measurements. Read against those criteria, the text was strong on
relevance and weak where the others likely scored:

- **Innovation:** the idea followed the builder guide's own starter kits 1 and 3, and its demo beats
  (a failed transfer replaced under a duplicate lock) were the guide's kit 3 almost word for word.
  Kits 1-4 needed no extra access, so they were probably the most crowded path, and invoice matching
  before payment is an established accounts-payable category.
- **Product integration:** it used Airwallex's basics (balances, FX, beneficiaries, transfers,
  webhooks). It touched none of the products the event was built to show off: cards and spend
  controls, connected accounts, agentic commerce, or the Visa and Metal tooling behind two of the
  four prizes.
- **Impact:** the text explained the mechanism and its safety, not the size of the problem. It never
  said what payment fraud through changed bank details costs, or who loses it.
- **Form:** about 2 400 characters in one field, with the concept behind a link a screener may never
  open.

**Rule:** before an idea screen, find its criteria; when none are published, write to these five.
Lead with impact in numbers, say in one sentence what is new compared with the organiser's own starter
kit, and go deep on the sponsor's newest product instead of its basics. Keep the text short enough to
be read whole, and do not leave the argument in a link.
