As Agreed

An agent that pays supplier invoices only as far as they match what was agreed.

Concept for the Airwallex Agentic Banking Hackathon · Starter kits 1 (Adaptive Treasury Controller) and 3 (Payment Ops Incident Commander), with reconciliation · 20 October 2026 · No code yet: the build starts on 25 October.

The problem

A finance team paying supplier invoices in several currencies rarely loses money on arithmetic. It loses it on the payment that should never have left:

a duplicate invoice a line nobody ordered an email saying “we changed our bank details” a transfer that fails quietly and is then paid twice an amount that drifts after FX and fees

The check: every invoice line, before any money moves

What was agreedpurchase orders and contracts
Who may be paidthe verified beneficiary record
The payment policy, in codeautonomous limit, duplicate lock, reserve floor, FX tolerance
matched
an agreed line supports it, no rule blocks it, the beneficiary is the verified one
paid through Airwallex within the autonomous limit
conflict
a rule blocks it: duplicate, changed beneficiary, over the limit, below the reserve floor
payment halts; a person decides
proposal
nothing that was agreed supports it
shown to the approver; never paid without approval
uncertain conversion
after FX and fees the payable amount drifts beyond tolerance
goes to the approver

Each line has exactly one state. Precedence: conflict > proposal > uncertain conversion > matched. A halt a rule fired stays a halt, whatever a model says later.

The demo

GoalPay this week’s six supplier invoices: USD, GBP and EUR, four suppliers.
Initial planA state for every line; FX, the SWIFT fee and the reserve floor priced in.
New informationA supplier emails new bank details. One transfer ends CANCELLED with a failure type.
Revised decisionThat supplier is held and verified out of band. The failed transfer is replaced under a duplicate lock; the original’s fate is recorded.
ResultBalances, payments and holds, and a reconciliation report: every paid line traces to its invoice, agreement and approval.

Built on Airwallex (sandbox)

  • Plan: balances/current, fx/rates/current, fx/quotes/create, fx/conversions/create
  • Pay: beneficiaries/schema, /validate, /create; transfers/create; US and GB local, EUR over SWIFT
  • Stage: simulation/deposit/create, simulation/transfers/{id}/transition, behind one interface
  • Reconcile: GET transfers and the payout webhooks (paid, failed, cancelled), signature checked and deduplicated

Who decides what

Code
amounts, limits, the reserve floor, duplicate protection, state transitions, every Airwallex call (typed tools, a new request id per operation)
Models
read invoices, contracts and emails, match lines, explain exceptions; a second model from a different family tries to refute every match
A person
every conflict, proposal and uncertain conversion; each approval bound to the amount, currency, beneficiary and the evidence shown

Measured, not claimed

no double paymenttested in code; one violation fails the build
no payment to an unverified beneficiarytested in code
nothing paid below the reserve floortested in code

A pre-registered error-injection run (duplicate invoice, wrong amount, changed beneficiary, unordered line, wrong currency, omitted fee, and clean lines) reports recall per error type, false alarms on clean lines and time to decision, with the thresholds locked before the first run and the results reported as they fall.
